HintParaphrase: «show» → «compare/illustrate»; name what is measured (preferred communication methods, in percentages), the two age groups. No dates on these charts → present simple, no trend verbs anywhere.

Sample: The two pie charts compare the preferred methods of communication for two age groups — 18–30 year olds and 50–65 year olds.

HintTwo stories, no numbers: (1) social media dominate among the young, whereas phone calls and email lead among the older group; (2) text messaging is the only method with similar shares in both groups.

Sample: Overall, social media account for nearly half of preferences among 18–30 year olds, whereas the older group favours phone calls and email. Text messaging is the only method used to a broadly similar extent by both age groups.

Єдина підказка · groupingBody 1 = the big swap: hotels (falling) vs private rentals (surging past them). Body 2 = the three smaller options, all declining (staying with friends, camping, other). Overview: rentals overtook hotels; everything else shrank. Remember: years → past simple + change language.
ІнструкціяПиши всю відповідь одразу (§1–§4, 150+ слів). Без таймера. Після чек-листа відкриється модель.

Чек-лист перед моделлю

§1 The two pie charts compare where tourists in one region chose to stay in 2010 and 2024.

§2 Overall, private rentals overtook hotels to become the most popular accommodation by 2024, while every other option became less common.

§3 Hotels accounted for just under half of all stays in 2010, at 48%, but this share fell markedly to 35% by 2024. Private rentals, by contrast, surged from 15% to 38% over the same period — the most dramatic change on the charts.
